What it is

The Leadmoz referral program lets every account holder earn from the customers they bring in. When a friend signs up through your referral link and pays for a qualifying plan, you earn 20% of every payment they make — forever, including renewals.

Joining costs nothing and no approval is needed: your personal referral link appears in the dashboard, ready to copy.

How it works

  • Open your dashboard — your referral link sits in the referral card and looks like https://leadmoz.com/auth?ref=…. Copy it and share it however you like.
  • When a friend signs up through your link, the signup appears on your dashboard the moment the account is created.
  • Each time that friend pays for a qualifying paid plan, you earn 20% of that payment — the first payment and every renewal.
  • Earnings show as Pending once a paid plan is approved, then are paid out after approval.

Rules

  • Self-referrals — same email or same device/IP — don’t count.
  • Qualifying paid plans only: no commissions on Free or Admin accounts.
  • A refund cancels the commission for that payment.
  • Describe Leadmoz honestly, don’t spam, and follow the Acceptable Use Policy — the standards that apply to your own outreach also apply to how you talk about Leadmoz.
