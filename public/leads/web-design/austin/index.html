How to find and approach web design prospects in Austin, plus a repeatable local outreach workflow for freelancers and small studios.

What actually works here

Lead with one concrete, verifiable improvement you can point to on the prospect's own site, such as a slow mobile load or a missing online booking step, instead of a generic redesign pitch. Check the page yourself before claiming a problem exists.

Service angle to lead with

Offer a small, fixed-scope entry service, such as a mobile-speed or booking-flow fix, so the first yes is low-risk and easy to evaluate.
